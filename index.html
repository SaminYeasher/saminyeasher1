DigiQueue - Virtual Queueing Mobile App

Developing a centralized, remote virtual queueing system to eliminate physical waiting lines outside university faculty offices. The Flutter mobile app allows students to join office-hour queues digitally and track their real-time position and estimated wait time without physically standing outside the door.

Technologies: Flutter, Dart, Mobile Application Development, Real-Time Queueing

LawAIr: Smart AI Legal Assistant

Developed an AI-based legal assistant that analyzes user queries and provides relevant legal insights using natural language processing techniques. Implemented text classification and information retrieval methods to match queries with appropriate legal information from the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 in both Bangla and English.

Technologies: Natural Language Processing, Text Classification, Information Retrieval, Bangla and English
